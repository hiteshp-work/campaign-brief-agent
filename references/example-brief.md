# Campaign Brief: AI Training Module — Credit Union ABM Sprint

**Verdict:** GO with a refinement — narrow the ICP to credit unions with 200–2,000 employees and lead with the compliance angle; the generic "AI training" pitch is too broad for this budget.
**Date:** 2026-09-27
*Example output from the campaign brief agent. Company details are illustrative; assumptions are labeled throughout.*

## 1. Objective
- Primary goal: 20 sales-accepted opportunities (SAOs) in 8 weeks
- Secondary goals: build a warmed ABM list of 400 target accounts; collect 150+ MQLs for nurture
- Primary success metric: SAOs (not MQLs — this budget can't afford vanity volume)

## 2. ICP Fit Check
- **Fit rating:** Strong (with the credit-union refinement)
- Target ICP: Heads of L&D, VP Operations, Chief People Officers at Canadian credit unions and mid-market banks, 200–2,000 employees
- Buying triggers this campaign leans on: annual compliance training cycles, branch staff turnover, digital banking rollouts requiring reskilling
- **Mismatches:** original "AI-powered training" framing appeals to innovation buyers, not the compliance-driven buyers who actually sign off — lead with audit readiness instead

## 3. Messaging
- **Value prop (one line):** Cut compliance training time in half while proving audit readiness — purpose-built for financial institutions.
- **Angle 1:** "Your next audit is coming. Will your training records survive it?" — CTA: See the audit-readiness checklist
- **Angle 2:** "Onboard branch staff 40% faster without adding trainers" — CTA: Watch the 3-min demo
- **Angle 3:** "The hidden cost of generic LMS tools in regulated banking" — CTA: Read the benchmark report
- **Objection to beat:** "We already have an LMS" — counter: position as the compliance layer that sits alongside it, not a rip-and-replace

## 6. Timeline
- **Weeks 1–2 (Build):** Finalize 400-account list, build landing page + report, legal sign-off on compliance claims, launch creative
- **Weeks 3–6 (Launch & optimize):** Weekly creative review; shift 20% of budget from worst to best channel at week 4
- **Weeks 7–8 (Evaluate):** SAO count vs 20 target; decision: scale to $100K/quarter, iterate angles, or kill
- **Slip risks:** legal approval on compliance claims (start week 1); account list quality (validate before spend)

## 7. KPIs & Measurement
- **Leading (weeks 1–2):** LinkedIn CTR > 0.7%, landing page CVR > 10%, cost per MQL < $250
- **Lagging:** 20 SAOs, ~$600K pipeline (assumes $30K avg deal), MQL→SAO rate > 15%
- **Attribution:** first-touch for channel credit, multi-touch for pipeline reporting; weekly SAO review with sales

## 8. Risks & Mitigations
1. Account list too small to spend $18K on LinkedIn efficiently → validate 400 accounts can support frequency; expand to adjacent titles if delivery stalls
2. Compliance claims blocked by legal → pre-clear 3 approved claim variants in week 1
3. Sales follow-up lag kills SAO conversion → agree the <24h SLA in writing before launch; shared dashboard
