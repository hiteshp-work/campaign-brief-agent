- Primary goal: [e.g. 25 sales-accepted opportunities]
- Secondary goals: [e.g. build ABM target list of 300 accounts]

- **Fit rating:** Strong / Partial / Weak

- Buying triggers this campaign leans on: [e.g. compliance audit cycle, branch expansion]

- **Value prop (one line):** [what it does, for whom, why it wins]
